Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

TETHER is made by Levi Minton. This page explains what the game stores, where it goes and how to get it removed. The short version: there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold to anyone.

On your device

Your progress, settings, gems, unlocks and the levels you make are saved on your iPhone. They stay there unless you use the online features below.

Online features (iCloud)

Publishing levels, friends, profiles, leaderboards and ghost races run on Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). The game never sees your Apple ID, email or real name. iCloud gives it an anonymous ID instead. When you use these features, the game stores:

  • the display name you choose and your friend code
  • your public profile: equipped pair, rank, stars, badge count, endless best and number of levels published
  • levels you publish, with their title and your display name
  • your best scores and the ghost replay of those runs (the path your orbs took)
  • friend requests and your friends list
  • likes and play counts on levels

Other players can see your display name, profile, published levels and scores. Your friends list is visible to you and the people on it.

Purchases

In-app purchases go through Apple. The game gets a receipt saying what you bought, never your payment details.

Children

The game doesn't ask for anyone's age, real name, email, location or contacts. The only thing a player types that others can see is their display name and level titles.

Removing your data

In the game, open Community, then Friends, then Profile, and tap Delete Online Data. That removes your profile, friend code, friends, scores and published levels from iCloud. You can also email support@leviminton.com and I'll do it for you. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
