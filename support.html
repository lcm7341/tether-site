Support

Something broken, a purchase that didn't show up, or a level you want taken down? Email support@leviminton.com and I'll get back to you. Include your iPhone model and what happened, and a screenshot if you have one.

A purchase didn't show up

Open the Shop, go to the GEMS tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Restore Purchases. That brings back the Supporter Pack and Knot & Bow. Gem packs are used up once bought, so if gems from a pack never arrived, email me with the date and the pack.

Online levels, friends and scores don't work

These use your iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud in Settings, then reopen the game.

Your data

To remove your profile, levels and scores from the online features, open Community, then Friends, then Profile, and tap Delete Online Data. The privacy policy lists everything that's stored.
